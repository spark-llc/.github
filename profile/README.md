We build technology that brings everyday mobility services together, making it easier for people and businesses to move.

| Area | What we focus on |
| --- | --- |
| **Spark Connect** | A mobility SuperApp connecting services in one experience. |
| **EV charging** | Charging discovery, connected charging experiences and roaming integrations. |
| **MiniApps** | Focused experiences for travel, parking, micromobility and vehicle services. |
| **Spark Fleet** | Corporate mobility spending, vehicle limits and operational visibility. |
